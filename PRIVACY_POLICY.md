# FitQuick Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 21, 2026
_Last updated: March 21, 2026_
**Version:** 1.0.0

FitQuick ("we," "our," or "us") is an AI-powered fitness companion app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

## Information We Collect

### Fitness Data (Stored Locally)
- Workout plans, exercise logs, progress records, and reminders stored on your device using Room database.

### AI-Powered Features
- When you request a personalised workout plan, your fitness goals and preferences are sent to **Google Gemini API**.
- If you use camera-based form checking, video frames are processed locally or sent to Gemini — no video is stored on our servers.

### Analytics and Crash Reporting
- **Firebase Analytics**: Aggregated app usage data (screens visited, features used). No personally identifiable information is collected.
- **Firebase Crashlytics**: Crash reports including device model, OS version, and error stack traces. No personal data is included.

### Advertising
- **Google AdMob**: Displays ads. AdMob may collect advertising ID and usage data per [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## How We Use Your Information

- Generate personalised workout plans via Gemini AI when requested.
- Track your workout progress locally.
- Fix bugs via crash reports.
- Serve advertisements via AdMob.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## Storage and Retention

- Fitness data is stored locally on your device.
- Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase per their policies.
- No SudarshanTechLabs server stores your fitness data.

## Data Security

- Local data is protected by Android's built-in storage encryption.
- Data sent to Firebase and Gemini is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS.

## Permissions Used

- **CAMERA**: Required for optional camera-based form checking.
- **INTERNET**: Required for AI features, analytics, crash reporting, and ads.
- **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE**: Used to check connectivity before network requests.
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS** (Android 13+): Required to send workout reminders.

## Your Rights and Controls

- Disable personalised ads via Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.
- Delete all local fitness data by uninstalling the app.
- Contact us to request deletion of any Firebase-linked data.

## Children's Privacy

FitQuick is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
