# Privacy Policy — FitQuick

**Last Updated:** March 21, 2026
**Effective Date:** March 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SudarshanTechLabs ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses,
and shares information when you use the **FitQuick** Android application ("the App").

By using FitQuick, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with
this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Profile Information:** Name (optional), age, height, weight, and fitness goals you enter
  to personalise your workout plans.
- **Workout Data:** Exercises completed, sets, reps, duration, and notes you log within the App.
- **Progress Photos:** Photos you choose to capture using the in-app camera feature. These are
  stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to our servers.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Analytics:** We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymised data about how features
  are used (e.g., which screens are viewed, session length). This data contains no personally
  identifiable information.
- **Crash Reports:** We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic
  information to help us fix bugs and improve stability. Crash reports may include device model,
  OS version, and app state at the time of the crash.
- **Advertising Identifiers:** If you interact with ads served by Google AdMob, Google may
  collect your Advertising ID and related data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

### 1.3 AI-Generated Content

When you request an AI-generated workout plan, a prompt describing your fitness goals and
preferences is sent to the **Google Gemini API** (operated by Google LLC). This prompt does not
include your name or contact details. Please review [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
for details on how Google processes API requests.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

- Generate personalised workout plans tailored to your goals and fitness level
- Display your workout history, progress charts, and achievements
- Send workout reminder notifications (only if you enable them)
- Diagnose crashes and improve app stability
- Understand aggregate feature usage to improve the App
- Serve relevant advertisements via Google AdMob

We **do not** sell your personal information to third parties.

---

## 3. Data Storage

- **Local Storage:** All workout data, profile information, and progress photos are stored
  locally on your device using a Room (SQLite) database and Android DataStore. This data does
  not leave your device except as described in Section 2.
- **No Account Required:** FitQuick does not require you to create an account or provide an
  email address. All core functionality works offline.
- **Backups:** If you have Android's automatic backup enabled, your app data may be backed up
  to your Google account as per Android's standard backup policy. You can disable this in your
  device settings.

## 5. Data Retention

- Data stored locally on your device is retained until you uninstall the App or clear the
  App's data via Android Settings.
- Anonymised analytics and crash data held by Firebase follows Google's standard retention
  schedules (typically 14 months for Analytics).

## 6. Children's Privacy

FitQuick is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal
information, please contact us at sudarshantechlabs@gmail.com and we will take steps to
delete that information.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Delete** your data (uninstalling the App deletes all locally stored data)
- **Opt out** of personalised advertising (via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads)

Since most data is stored locally on your device, you can exercise these rights directly
by clearing app data or uninstalling the App.
